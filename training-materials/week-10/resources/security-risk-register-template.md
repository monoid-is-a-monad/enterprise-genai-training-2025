## Guidance

1. Assign unique Threat IDs (e.g., `TM-2025-001`).
2. Rate impact/likelihood using agreed enterprise scale.
3. Describe existing controls and identify gaps.
4. Capture mitigation plan with clear ownership and due date.
5. Update status as `Open`, `In Progress`, `Mitigated`, or `Accepted`.
6. Link to evidence (PRs, policy docs, dashboards) for auditability.

## Review Cadence

- Update register during Week 10 labs.
- Review in Responsible AI council meetings and CAB sessions.
- Archive completed registers under `docs/risk-registers/` for traceability.
