# Lab 03 – Governance Automation & Audit Evidence Packaging (Solutions)

> Instructor reference for validating governance automation and briefing deliverables.

---

## 1. Evidence Manifest

**Expect:**
- `artifacts/governance-evidence-manifest.json` listing locations for policies, evaluations, deployments, incidents, approvals, and scorecard.
- Accurate paths and release identifiers; timestamps within 24h.
- Explanation of how manifest is generated (script/notebook, schedule).

---

## 2. Audit Runbook Updates

**Checklist:**
- `resources/compliance-audit-runbook.md` appended with Week 10 automation notes.
- Stakeholder responsibilities and cadence refreshed.
- Pre-audit checklist updated with status markers.

---

## 3. Scorecard Snapshot & Metrics

**Review:**
- Snapshot file (CSV/Markdown) showing latest Responsible AI metrics.
- Scorecard dataset updated with current values, owners, evidence links.
- Any metric outside target flagged with mitigation plan.

---

## 4. Governance Charter & Decision Log

- `docs/governance/week-10-charter.md` updated with council membership, decisions, and review cadence.
- Action items assigned with due dates.
- Alignment with Responsible AI council roles (lesson content).

---

## 5. Executive Brief

**Evidence:**
- Brief stored in `artifacts/week10-executive-brief.md` (or similar) covering status, risks, next steps.
- Summaries align with scorecard and risk register.
- Distribution plan documented (stakeholders, channels).

---

## 6. Submission Package

Ensure learners provide:
- Evidence manifest
- Audit runbook diff
- Scorecard snapshot
- Governance charter updates
- Executive brief
- Completed Responsible AI checklist and stakeholder brief template

---

## Instructor Notes

- Encourage collecting sign-offs acknowledging receipt of executive brief.
- Verify evidence manifest feeds into compliance vault or ticketing workflow.
- Reinforce coordinating with Week 9 incident processes to keep governance artifacts in sync.
